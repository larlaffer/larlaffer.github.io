Concert Preview

Celebrate 250!

Sunday June 7, 2026, 3:00 PM
Anderson Community Theatre

The Anderson Community Band (ACB), conducted by John Fecker, is pleased to announce the 2025-26 ACB Season Finale, Celebrate 250! The concert will be held on Sunday June 7th, at 3:00 PM in the beautiful Anderson Center Theater; 7850 Five Mile Rd, Cincinnati, OH 45230. The performance is open to the public and free of charge. No tickets are necessary.

The ACB will perform a variety program of American works for band. The program will include John Williams' Midway March and the Symphonic Suite from Superman. The American Overture for Band by Joseph Jenkins and Fanfare Politeia by Kimberly Archer will also be performed. The music of George M Cohan will be heard in a lively arrangement titled Star Spangled Spectacular. Featured soloists for this event will include ACB band members David Smith (tuba) and Emily Smith (piccolo) performing The Elephant and the Fly. David and Emily graduated from The Ohio State University in 2003 and Miami University in 2008 and are the band directors for Batavia Schools. The ACB Dixieland quintet will also be heard in a rousing arrangement of When the Saints Go Marching In.
